- Harmony:fitting into the social and natural world as it is; understanding, appreciating, and accommodating rather than changing, directing, or exploiting it (world at peace, unity with nature, protecting the environment).
- Embeddedness: people as embedded in the collectivity; meaning through a shared way of life and group goals; maintaining social order, tradition, and security; restraining actions that disrupt solidarity (social order, respect for tradition, security, obedience, wisdom).
- Hierarchy: hierarchical, ascribed roles ensure responsible behavior; unequal distribution of power, roles, and resources is legitimate and expected (social power, authority, humility, wealth).
- Mastery: active self-assertion to master, direct, and change the natural and social environment to reach goals (ambition, success, daring, competence).
- Affective Autonomy: society encourages individuals to pursue affectively positive experience for themselves (pleasure, exciting life, varied life).
- Intellectual Autonomy: society encourages individuals to pursue their own ideas and intellectual directions independently (broadmindedness, curiosity, creativity).
- Egalitarianism: people recognize one another as moral equals sharing basic interests; voluntary cooperation and concern for everyone's welfare (equality, social justice, responsibility, help, honesty).
